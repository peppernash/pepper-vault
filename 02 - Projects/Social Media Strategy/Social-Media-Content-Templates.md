## 🎨 **VISUAL CONTENT GUIDELINES**

### **LinkedIn Visual Style:**
- **Colors:** Professional blues, grays, Yale Appliance brand colors
- **Fonts:** Clean, modern fonts (Arial, Helvetica, or brand fonts)
- **Images:** High-quality, professional photos and graphics
- **Charts/Data:** Clean infographics with clear data visualization
- **Personal Photos:** Professional headshots and workplace candids

### **Instagram Visual Style:**
- **Colors:** Soccer team colors, energetic brights (green, blue, orange)
- **Fonts:** Bold, energetic fonts for headers, readable fonts for details
- **Images:** Action shots, training moments, celebration photos
- **Videos:** Dynamic movement, clear demonstrations, good lighting
- **Graphics:** Simple, bold graphics with key points highlighted

## 📈 **ENGAGEMENT BOOSTERS**

### **LinkedIn Engagement Tactics:**
- **Ask questions** that encourage professional discussion
- **Share contrarian views** (respectfully) on industry trends
- **Tag relevant people** when sharing insights about their companies
- **Create polls** about marketing strategies or industry preferences
- **Comment thoughtfully** on industry leaders' posts before posting your own

### **Instagram Engagement Tactics:**
- **Use Instagram Stories** polls, questions, and quiz features
- **Create "Save-worthy" content** that coaches and parents will bookmark
- **Respond to every comment** with personalized responses
- **Share user-generated content** from players and parents
- **Go live occasionally** for Q&A sessions or real-time coaching tips

---

**These templates provide a foundation for consistent, valuable content that builds your dual personal brand while saving time on content creation.**
