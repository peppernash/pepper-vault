**10% - Personal Professional** *(1 post/week)*

### **Content Formats:**
- **Text posts** with insights and commentary
- **Image carousels** with data/trends

- **Long-form articles** (monthly thought leadership)

### **Optimal Posting Times:**
- **Tuesday-Thursday: 8-10 AM EST** (morning business hours)
