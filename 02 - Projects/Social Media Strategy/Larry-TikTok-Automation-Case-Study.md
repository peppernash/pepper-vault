# 🎯 Larry TikTok Automation Case Study - Key Learnings

**Source:** Oliver Henry & Larry (AI Agent)  
**Results:** 500K+ views, $588/month MRR, automated TikTok content creation

---

## 🔑 **CRITICAL SUCCESS FORMULA DISCOVERED:**

### **Winning Hook Pattern:**
**[Another person] + [conflict/doubt] → showed them AI → they changed their mind**

**Examples that worked:**
- "My landlord said I can't change anything so I showed her what AI thinks it could look like" → **234K views**
- "I showed my mum what AI thinks our living room could be" → **167K views**
- "My landlord wouldn't let me decorate until I showed her these" → **147K views**

**Failed hooks (self-focused):**
- "Why does my flat look like a student loan" → 905 views
- "See your room in 12+ styles before you commit" → 879 views

## 🛠️ **TECHNICAL SETUP:**

### **Infrastructure:**
- **OpenClaw** - AI agent framework (what Pat already has!)
- **Old gaming PC with Ubuntu** - Local hosting
- **Claude Anthropic** - AI model choice
- **OpenAI gpt-image-1.5** - Image generation ($0.50/post, $0.25 with Batch API)
- **Postiz** - TikTok API posting (uploads as drafts)

### **Content Format:**
- **6 slides exactly** (TikTok's engagement sweet spot)
- **Portrait orientation** (1024x1536, not landscape)
- **Consistent architecture** across all slides - only style changes
- **Text overlay on slide 1** with hook
- **Max 5 hashtags** (TikTok's current limit)

### **Critical Technical Learnings:**
- **Lock the architecture** - Same room dimensions, window positions, furniture in every prompt
- **Only change style** - Wall colors, decor, lighting between slides
- **"iPhone photo" + "realistic lighting"** prompts for authenticity
- **Include everyday items** - TV, mugs, remote control for relatability

---

## 📋 **THE WORKFLOW:**

1. **Larry generates** images with locked architecture prompts
2. **Adds text overlays** with proper sizing (6.5% font size)
3. **Writes captions** using proven formulas
4. **Uploads to TikTok as drafts** via Postiz API
5. **Human adds trending music** and publishes (60 seconds work)

**Total time:** Larry does 95% of work (15-30 minutes), human does 5% (60 seconds)

## 🔥 **KEY SUCCESS FACTORS:**

### **Content Psychology:**
✅ **Focus on other people's reactions** not product features  
✅ **Create conflict/tension** that gets resolved  
✅ **Tell micro-stories** that viewers can visualize  
✅ **Make it about human moments** not technical specs  

### **Technical Excellence:**
✅ **Obsessive consistency** in image generation  
✅ **Proper formatting** for mobile viewing  
✅ **Readable text overlays** with correct sizing  
✅ **Authentic-looking content** not obviously AI-generated  

### **System Thinking:**
✅ **Document everything** in skill files  
✅ **Learn from every failure** and update processes  
✅ **Compound improvements** over time  
✅ **Automate 95% of work** while keeping human oversight for creativity
