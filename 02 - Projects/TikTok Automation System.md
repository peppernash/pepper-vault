## 🔑 **The Winning Formula**

**Larry's Proven Pattern:**  
**[Another person] + [conflict/doubt] → showed them solution → they changed their mind**

### **Examples that WORKED:**
- "My landlord said I can't change anything so I showed her what AI thinks it could look like" → **234K views**
- "I showed my mum what AI thinks our living room could be" → **167K views**

### **Examples that FAILED (self-focused):**
- "See your room in 12+ styles before you commit" → 879 views
- "The difference between $500 and $5000 taste" → 2,671 views

## 🏗️ **Yale Appliance Adaptation**

1. **Slide 1:** Hook with text overlay
2. **Slides 2-3:** "Before" kitchen (outdated appliances)
3. **Slides 4-6:** "After" with Yale appliance packages

### **Technical Specs:**
- **Portrait orientation:** 1024x1536 pixels
- **6 slides exactly** (TikTok engagement sweet spot)
- **Text overlay on slide 1 only**
- **Max 5 hashtags**
- **Consistent kitchen architecture** across slides
